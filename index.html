References & limitations

What this tool does and does not do

  • Colorimetric math and ICC profile conversions are separate paths. They are labelled separately throughout, because they answer different questions.
  • CMYK is not a universal colour space. Every separation shown here is defined by the profiles, rendering intent, colour-management module and options in force at that moment.
  • A soft proof is not a contract proof. Browser rendering cannot certify a display or a print process. Screen calibration, viewing conditions and the output profile all affect what you see.
  • FOGRA51 and FOGRA52 are informative examples of standardized print conditions, not substitutes for the actual job profile or a press contract.
  • No universal total ink coverage limit is assumed. Coverage is a calculated sum; it is compared only against a limit you set or documented profile and process data.
  • Profile processing stays local. Profile bytes are held in this browser session only and are never uploaded.

Measurement scope

ChromaLab does not take measurements. Spectral measurement claims are out of scope unless spectral data and the relevant measurement geometry and observer inputs are supplied.

Industry sources

ICC

Device colour management combines profiles through a Colour Management Module. Output profiles can describe CMYK and other device colour encodings, and rendering intent controls how out-of-gamut colours are handled. The ICC identifies Bradford as a widely used D50↔D65 chromatic adaptation approach.

ICC colour-management FAQ

LittleCMS

LittleCMS describes itself as an ICC colour-management module supporting profile transforms, soft proofing, gamut checking, black-point compensation and multicolor workflows.

LittleCMS colour engine — lcms-wasm

Fogra & ECI

Fogra's characterisation-data reference lists FOGRA51 for sheet-fed coated offset and FOGRA52 for uncoated stock, and explains that profile separation results may vary even when profiles use the same characterisation data. Its data redistribution terms require the data to remain unmodified and Fogra to be identified as the source. ECI describes FOGRA51/52 as conditions developed for ISO 12647-2:2013 and notes the importance of D50 viewing and measurement mode M1 when optical brighteners are involved.

Fogra characterisation data · ECI offset standards · ICC profile registry: PSO Uncoated v3 / FOGRA52
